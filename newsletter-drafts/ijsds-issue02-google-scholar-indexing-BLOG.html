IJSDS Newsletter · Issue 02 · Editorial update

An apology — and a Google Scholar update

Published 30 September 2026 · IJSDS Editorial Office · 3 min read

To authors who wrote to ask about Google Scholar indexing: we are sorry that our response took longer than it should have.

We should have acknowledged those enquiries sooner and provided a clear update. We appreciate your patience, and we are improving how we track and respond to indexing questions.

Current visibility update: a Google Scholar source search for the International Journal of Social Work and Development Studies showed about 36 results when this update was prepared. This means that at least 36 IJSDS article records are currently discoverable in Google Scholar.

This is encouraging progress for the visibility of research published with IJSDS. It does not mean that every article will appear in the same way or at the same time: Google Scholar's discovery and record-refresh processes can vary by article.

How to check your article

  • Search Google Scholar using your article's exact title in quotation marks.
  • If needed, search the article title together with “IJSDS” or the author surname.
  • Check that the result links to the correct article page or PDF and displays the correct title and authorship.

You can begin with the IJSDS source search in Google Scholar. If your published article does not yet appear, please email editor@ijsds.org with the article title, DOI (if available), and its IJSDS web link. Our editorial office will check the record and follow up.

Thank you for publishing with IJSDS and for holding us to the standard of communication you deserve.
