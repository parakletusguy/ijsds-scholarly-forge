# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Two equal audiences on the public site:
- **Readers**: researchers, students, practitioners and policymakers looking for social work and development research, mostly arriving from search engines, Google Scholar and DOI links.
- **Authors**: academics (largely African, many in Nigeria) deciding whether IJSDS is a credible, indexed, open-access home for their manuscript, then submitting.

## Product Purpose
IJSDS (International Journal of Social Work and Development Studies, ISSN 3115-6940 / eISSN 3115-6932) is a peer-reviewed, open-access journal. The public site must let readers find and cite research quickly, and convince authors the journal is legitimate: verifiable facts (ISSN, Crossref DOIs, open access licence, peer review, editorial board), never unverifiable marketing numbers.

## Brand Personality
Scholarly, credible, quietly confident. Content first. Authority comes from clear information and real articles, not decoration.

## Anti-references
- SaaS landing pages: hero metrics, stat counters, icon-card grids, gradient accents.
- Predatory-journal tells: inflated impact numbers, "fast publication" promises, badge clutter.
- Unverifiable claims of any kind.

## Design Principles
1. Show the research: real articles and their metadata lead the page.
2. Credibility through facts that can be checked (ISSN, DOI, licence, indexing, board).
3. Restraint: one accent colour (the terracotta), serif headlines, generous whitespace.
4. Every element earns its place; remove anything that repeats or cannot be verified.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast; keyboard reachable controls; respects reduced motion.
